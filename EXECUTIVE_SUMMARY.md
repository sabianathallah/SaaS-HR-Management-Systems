## 🎁 WHAT DOES IT DO?

### 12 Core Features:

1. **Attendance** - Clock in/out with GPS & photo verification
2. **Leave Management** - Request/approve with balance tracking
3. **Overtime** - Request & calculate overtime pay automatically
4. **Payroll** - Auto-generate payroll, direct bank transfer
5. **Hybrid Work** - Manage WFH/office schedules per employee
6. **Reports** - Real-time dashboards, analytics, export to Excel
7. **Mobile App** - iOS/Android with offline functionality
8. **Directory** - Central employee database with profiles
9. **Notifications** - Real-time alerts via email, SMS, in-app
10. **Organization** - Department, team, manager structure
11. **Audit** - Compliance tracking, audit logs, data security
12. **Multi-tenant** - Support many companies in 1 platform

**Translation:** Company can manage everything about employees in ONE system.
